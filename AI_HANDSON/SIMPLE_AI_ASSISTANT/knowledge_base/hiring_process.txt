The company's hiring process consists of five stages: resume screening, an online assessment, a technical interview, a managerial interview, and an HR discussion. 
Candidates who successfully complete all stages receive an offer letter within seven working days. 
The standard probation period for new employees is six months.
Employees can refer candidates through the internal employee referral portal. A referral bonus of INR 25,000 is provided after the referred candidate successfully completes three months of employment. 
Candidates who are not selected can reapply for the same position after six months.

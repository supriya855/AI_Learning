All new employees must submit their identity proof, educational certificates, previous employment documents, PAN card, and bank account details during onboarding.
Employees must complete mandatory information security and workplace ethics training within the first 30 days of joining.
The standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. 
Employees are eligible to work from home for two days per week with prior managerial approval.
 The HR department conducts performance reviews twice a year, in June and December.

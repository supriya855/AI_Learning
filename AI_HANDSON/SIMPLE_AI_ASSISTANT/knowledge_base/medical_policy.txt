The company provides group health insurance coverage of INR 5,00,000 per year for employees, their spouses, and up to two dependent children.
Employees may include their parents in the insurance plan by paying an additional annual premium. 
Cashless treatment is available at approved network hospitals.
Employees can claim up to INR 15,000 annually for eligible outpatient consultations, prescribed medicines, and diagnostic tests. 
Medical reimbursement requests must be submitted within 30 days of treatment, along with original bills and prescriptions. 
Approved claims are processed within 15 working days.

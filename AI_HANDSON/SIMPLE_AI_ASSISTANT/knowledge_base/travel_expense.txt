Employees travelling for official business must obtain managerial approval before booking transportation and accommodation. 
Domestic hotel expenses are reimbursed up to INR 4,000 per night, while international accommodation is reimbursed up to INR 10,000 per night. 
Economy-class flight tickets are permitted for official business travel.
Employees receive a daily meal allowance of INR 1,000 for domestic travel and INR 3,000 for international travel. 
Travel reimbursement claims must be submitted within 15 days of completing the trip, along with invoices, boarding passes, and payment receipts.
Approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 working days.

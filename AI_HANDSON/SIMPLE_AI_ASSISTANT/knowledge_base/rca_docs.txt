The Resource Centre provides employees with laptops, monitors, testing devices, software licenses, and technical equipment required for project development.
Employees must raise a resource request through the internal service portal. 
Standard equipment requests are processed within three working days.
Employees are responsible for maintaining the equipment assigned to them. Any damage, loss, or malfunction must be reported to the Resource Centre within 24 hours. 
All company-issued equipment must be returned during the employee's exit clearance process.
Personal installation of unauthorized software is strictly prohibited.
